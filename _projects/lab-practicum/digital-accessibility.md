    **Problem.** Accessibility resources at HGSE are decentralized. Faculty Coordinators (FCs) and staff often are unsure where to start when designing or reviewing a course for accessibility.

    **Audience.** FCs & staff / teaching teams who design and review courses at HGSE.

    ### Scope · updated

    | In scope (pilot) | Out of scope |
    | --- | --- |
    | Pilot **training module in Canvas** | Changing accessibility policy / standards at HGSE |
    | What accessibility is; why it matters (with student feedback) | Large-scale accessibility needs analysis across HGSE |
    | Checklist + tool-specific resources for **Google Docs, Slides, and Canvas** (available while staff are working) | Evaluation of impact over time |
    | Scaffolding by accessibility challenge | Accessibility in general (beyond digital course materials) |
    | Workflow ending in **Cidi Labs + UDOIT** | Faculty-related support/training (per original charter) |

  title: Capstone Product Overview
  align: left
  body: |
    **Concept.** A **Canvas training module** we are **piloting first** — practical accessibility support for course staff, not a finished product yet.

    **Who it’s for.** Faculty Coordinators (FCs) and staff / teaching teams building and reviewing HGSE courses.

    ### What’s in it (current plan)

    - What accessibility is
    - Why it matters, grounded in student feedback
    - One resource each for **Google Docs**, **Slides**, and **Canvas**, available while staff are working
    - Scaffolding by accessibility challenge
    - A checklist
    - A workflow that ends in **Cidi Labs** and **UDOIT**
    - Ways to increase visibility of accessibility work

    ### How it fits their workflow

    Resources sit next to the tools people already use (Docs / Slides / Canvas). The module points to a clear path: learn the basics → apply the checklist while building → finish with Cidi Labs + UDOIT.

    ### Open questions / next steps

    - Set learning outcomes
    - Draft module content and page flow
    - Build a one-page Canvas module template
    - Prototype in Canvas + Google Slides (~11/12)
    - Gather feedback and iterate (~11/19) before the **Dec 3 Gallery Walk**
