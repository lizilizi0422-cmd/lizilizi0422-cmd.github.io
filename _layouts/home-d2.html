Hi, I’m Ziqi, nice to meet you.

I’m a learning designer studying at the Harvard Graduate School of Education, with a background in Industrial Design from the Rhode Island School of Design and coursework in education at Brown University.

My work moves between toy and play design, early childhood art education, and EdTech UX. Right now I’m researching digital accessibility and building FindMy Voice, a bilingual AI companion for Chinese immigrant teens, with AI vibe coding.

I’m based in Boston and open to remote work.
